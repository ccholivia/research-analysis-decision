03 / APPLY

A reliable research workflow

01

Frame the question

Define your outcome, predictors, estimand or prediction target, and hypothesis.

02

Inspect the data

Review measurement levels, study design, missing data, outliers, class balance and leakage.

03

Select and validate

Check assumptions. For ML/DL, split data by participant or time when needed, tune on training data only, and evaluate held-out performance.

04

Report transparently

Report effect sizes and intervals for inference; appropriate metrics, uncertainty, and external validation for prediction.

Inference ≠ prediction. A high-accuracy model does not establish causation. A significant p-value does not guarantee practical importance. Study design and validation matter more than model complexity.
